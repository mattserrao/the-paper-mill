The Paper MillMill

Run a recycled paperboard mill from truck to warehouse. Old corrugated boxes arrive by the truckload, get pulped, cleaned and thickened, and your paper machine turns them into giant reels that the winder cuts into rolls for customers. Keep everything flowing, spend your profit on upgrades, and ride out upsets: breakdowns, fires, storms and the occasional pigeon.
