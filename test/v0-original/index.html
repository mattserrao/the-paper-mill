Auto keeps each area matched to the paper machine: pulper feed tracks machine demand plus a correction toward the chest level target, truck arrivals and crews follow from that. Drag any slider to take that area to manual; press its AUTO chip or "Balance all" to hand it back. Production is speed × basis weight, scaled so 23m at 2,736 fpm and 24.55 lb gives 34.8 t/h, the calibration median. Chaos events change the same flows: a machine incident stops the reel, stock prep incidents stop the pulper or cut yield, warehouse incidents cut crews, docks or trucks. Money: rolls are paid for when an outbound truck leaves (grade price × tons). OCC is paid for when it is unloaded, energy and chemicals per ton made, wages per crew member per hour, plus $4,000/h mill overhead and a repair bill for each disaster. Below −$1.5M the mill goes bankrupt. Assumptions: 22 t OCC per inbound truck, 88% pulper yield, 300 t chest, 30 t/h per forklift driver, 25 rolls/h per loader, 20 rolls per outbound truck.
